# Cold DM template (Twitter/X)

**When to use:** Only for developers who have *publicly* posted about being burned by an API deprecation, breaking change, or surprise vendor sunset. Reference their specific incident — never send this generic. Send manually, a few per day at most. If they don't reply, no follow-up.

---

**Template (3 sentences, personalize the bracket):**

> Saw your post about [the specific incident — e.g. "the Stripe webhook change eating your Tuesday"] — I built a tool after the exact same thing happened to me: it reads every changelog/status feed in your stack and only pings you when something will actually break you, with deadlines as countdowns. Would you try it and tell me where it falls short? Happy to set you up with 3 months of Pro free — no card, and blunt feedback is the only payment I want: https://upstream-pi.vercel.app

---

**Rules for use:**

- The first clause must prove you actually read their post. If you can't name their specific pain, don't send it.
- Never send to someone who hasn't posted about this problem. This is a relevance filter, not a spray list.
- If they reply with anything, respond like a person, not a funnel — answer the question they asked, even if it's "no thanks."
- Track sends manually (a simple list). Stop entirely if reply rate is under ~10% — the template is wrong or the targeting is.
